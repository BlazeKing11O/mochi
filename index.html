Welcome to Kokoro Mochi. "Kokoro" means heart in Japanese, and that's exactly what we put into every piece. We're a small mochi shop on a mission to make the softest, freshest mochi you've ever tasted.

We make everything by hand, in small batches, with real ingredients, because mochi this soft doesn't happen on an assembly line. New flavors are always on the way, so come hungry and come back often.

From Our Hands to Yours

Every Kokoro mochi starts as soft, steamed rice dough. We fill, shape, and wrap each piece by hand, one at a time, in small batches so everything reaches you fresh. It takes longer than the machine way, but that care is what gives you the perfect pillowy bite.

Why Choose Kokoro Mochi

Anyone can sell mochi. We set out to make the kind you can't stop thinking about. Our ingredients come from the regions known for growing the best, and nothing artificial ever goes in: no artificial colors, no artificial flavors, no shortcuts. Once you've tried Kokoro, store-bought mochi just won't compare.
